## 2026-09-23 — dilatational rings, from the "Inception doesn't end, it stops" poster exercise

Idea sparked while thumbnailing the dilatational (circle-based) layout for the line "Inception doesn't end. It stops.":

- Circle 1 — "Inception" — rotating slowly.
- Circle 2 — "doesn't end" — spinning fast, like an infinity loop.
- Circle 3 — camera pushes in; the spin stops exactly on "it stops," landing as a static frame.

The idea: motion itself performs the line's meaning (spin = "doesn't end," the stop = "it stops") rather than the words just describing it. Worth revisiting once this beat reaches Phase 3 (kinetic typography decision table) and Phase 4 (motion as direction) — test whether it survives the mute test and isn't just a neat idea in the head.
